## 2. Problems to know about before anything is reused

- **The material breaks your rulings.**
  - **"Dún"** is the example verb in several of your own worksheets: An Aimsir Láithreach 2, An Aimsir Fháistineach, Present Tense DAOINE FÁSTA, and Gramadach na Gaeilge 1 and 2.
  - **"Clois"** appears in about 10 files, **"-amar"** in 3, and **"bord"** in the first-declension table and the preposition table.
- **Four outside files use the non-Ulster mutation** after a preposition with "an" ("ar an mbus", "faoin gcrann"). The Achoimre summary and the Dioplóma notes teach it as the rule, which contradicts what the app teaches.
- **There are real errors, so nothing can be copied across unchecked.**
  - The 2005 declension set has "chun an háite", "cois an sléibhe" and "cóta mo mháthair".
  - The Br. Saor tables are scrambled.
  - The imperative sheet has wrong plurals.
  - "Suí" is given as a verb root in five files.
  - "caol: i, o" appears twice.
  - "An Aimsir Fháistineac1" is actually a past-tense exercise with the wrong title.
- **Some content is unsuitable for pupils.** This includes parts of the 2003 conversation notes, the Women's Aid leaflet, and dated or offensive health labels in the old dictionaries.
- **Place names need checking** on logainm.ie before any are used. "Áth An Mhuillean" should be "Áth an Mhuilinn". The notes have "Droim an Tí" but the app has "Droimeann Tí".

**There are also two errors in the app itself, and they are mine.**
1. The numbers lesson in the grammar section teaches "Beirt dhochtúirí". It should be **"beirt dochtúirí"**: after "beirt", d, t and s are not lenited. Your Year 8 unit already has "beirt deartháireacha" correctly.
2. Lessons on the genitive of first-declension nouns are filed under the second declension (d2), and the first-declension section (d1) teaches only plurals.

## 3. A sensible order for the material

Grammar is arranged here as one path, from Year 8 to A2:

1. **Foundations (Year 8):**
   - question words
   - numbers
   - days, months and dates
   - colours
   - "Tá mé i mo shuí"
   - "Is maith leis / léi"
   - "Tá + person + ag + verbal noun + sa + place"
   - classroom commands
2. **The noun (GCSE to AS):**
   - the article and gender
   - lenition and eclipsis
   - possessives and "s'agam"
   - plurals, with the weak-or-strong plural test
3. **The verb (GCSE to AS).** The imperative comes first, because it gives the root, then the past, present and future. The conditional comes *before* the past habitual, because the habitual endings are the conditional's without the f. Then the autonomous form in all five tenses.
4. **Linking sentences (AS):**
   - dependent clauses ("gur" and "nár"; "go" and "nach")
   - relative clauses
   - the copula for identification and emphasis ("Is é Seán ...", "Is ar maidin a ...")
5. **Accuracy (A2):**
   - the cases
   - the declensions and all six uses of the genitive
   - the vocative
   - weak and strong plurals
   - prepositions and prepositional pronouns
6. **Comhrá and writing (AS):**
   - opinion phrases
   - idioms
   - review language
   - agreeing and disagreeing

## 4. What would make the app richer, ranked by value against effort

| # | Section | Where it comes from | Value against effort |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Question words** (about 7 lessons). This also feeds Comhrá, where pupils must ask questions as well as answer them. | Míreanna ceisteacha (Mona Ní Dhálaigh) | High value, low effort |
| 2 | **Dependent clauses** ("Tá a fhios agam gur dhruid sé ..."; "Deir sé go n-imríonn sé ...") | the "cass" drill tables; Gramadach na Gaeilge 1 | High value, medium effort |

### New lessons inside existing sections

- **Verbs:**
  - verbs that shorten when an ending is added (oscail becomes osclaím, imir becomes imríonn, inis becomes inseoidh, labhair becomes labhróidh)
  - one-syllable verbs ending in -igh (ním, léim, suím; nífidh)
  - negative questions with "Nár" and "Nach"
  - more Ulster forms: tchífidh, bhéarfaidh, níor dhúirt, gabh
- **Nouns:**
  - the genitive after words of quantity: "a lán airgid", "roinnt eolais"
  - the genitive after chun, cois, dála, timpeall and trasna
  - the "weak or strong plural?" test, which makes the genitive plural predictable
  - a "Which declension?" recognition table
  - full tables for about 180 nouns, once the spreadsheets are corrected
- **Numbers:**
  - bliain, uair and pingin after numbers ("trí bliana", "ceithre huaire")
  - counting people above ten
  - years
- **Copula:** "Is é Seán an múinteoir"; "Is liom an mála"; "Is ar maidin a tháinig sé".
- **Adjectives:**
  - plurals ("na fir mhóra")
  - comparison with the copula
  - "an cóta s'agam"

### Comhrá AS

1. **A bank of opinion phrases** for every topic. Examples: "tagaim leis an mhéid sin", "tá mé idir dhá chomhairle faoi sin", and a scale from "Aontaím amach is amach" to "Easaontaím amach is amach". High value, low effort.
2. **New follow-up questions** for the 16 topics, rewritten in Ulster Irish, for example "Dá mbeadh do rogha saoire agat, cá rachfá?". High value, low effort.
3. **Three to five idioms per topic from *Deismir***, as optional tiles for "extra marks". Examples: "is fearr an tsláinte ná na táinte"; "ní ar bharr copóg a gheibhtear an t-airgead". This needs your sign-off, a credit and, beyond a handful, permission.
4. **Review phrases** for the TG4, films and streaming topic, for example "Ní raibh an aisteoireacht thar mholadh beirte".
5. **An account of last weekend in the past tense.** The current weekend topic uses the present habitual only.
6. **An agree-or-disagree practice mode** built on new statements written for today's teenagers in Northern Ireland.

### Year 8

- **Days of the week and full dates.** The Year 8 units have no "Dé Luain" and no "an ceathrú lá d'Iúil".
- **Colours:** "Tá geansaí gorm orm".
- **Counties and towns**, with the forms after "go" and "i" ("go hArd Mhacha", "i gContae an Dúin"), and what Armagh place names mean. Colleagues' files; check every name on logainm.ie.
- **A "Cad atá ar siúl?" sentence builder:** "Tá an gasúr ag rith sa pháirc".
- **Classroom commands**, using "druid", not "dún".
- **"Bím i mo shuí ar a seacht"**, and "Is maith leis / léi".
- **The short bedtime poem about irregular verbs**, as audio: "D'ith mé mo shuipéar agus d'ól mé mo thae ...".

### New exercise types (the app currently has five)

The notes use six more:
- matching
- translating from English
- finding and fixing the error
- sorting by gender or case
- a passage with gaps, using a word bank
- enriching a plain sentence with adjectives ("fuar fliuch feanntach")

The first three would add the most.

### Teaching devices for the hints

- Your mnemonics: "Barbie caught dopey friends going mad publicly, silly twits" (the letters that take h); "Scallions smell spicy in stew" (sc, sm, sp and st resist it).
- The "Group A / Group B" labels for irregular verbs.
- A reference card for each tense that sets out all four sets of endings in one grid (short or long verb, broad or slender ending).
