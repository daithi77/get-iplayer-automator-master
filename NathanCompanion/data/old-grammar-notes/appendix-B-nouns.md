# Old grammar notes: nouns and structures

Scope: the article, gender, mutations, plurals, the five declensions and the genitive, adjectives, numbers and dates, pronouns, prepositions, the copula, relative clauses and question words. Read-only analysis. Nothing in the app repository was changed.

Sources read: every file listed below in `oldgram/txt/`, plus the original `.htm` pages and their surviving image folders in `Grammar/` (the relative-clause, counting-people and calendar GIFs are still there and are readable), the Office metadata of each original (author, last saved by, dates), the app outline and the lesson JSON for `d1` to `d5`, `sealbhach`, `copail`, `uimhreacha`, `reamhfhocail-simpli` and `aidiachtai`, and the Year 8 `units.json`.

---

## 0. Headlines

1. **About half of this material is not the teacher's.** Every file whose metadata says "Valued Gateway Client" (March 2004) is a saved web page: two different sites, "Irish Language Resources" (mbm@indigo.ie; noun list taken from *Progress in Irish*) and a personal grammar site at homepage.tinet.ie/~kuichinneide (© 2000), plus pages from "Daltaí na Gaeilge" and a Munster-flavoured resource titled "Cruinneas na Gaeilge". Several more are by colleagues (Gerald/Gerard McKeown, "godochartaigh", R. Tierney, "... Ní Dhálaigh") or come from a 2005 slide set by "Cillian Breatnach". Only six files carry the teacher's own name ("Dith Murray", November 2008). Treat the rest as ideas to rewrite, not text to copy.
2. **The biggest ruling breach is eclipsis after preposition + article.** Four external files teach `ar an mbus`, `faoin gcrann`, `ar an gcíste`, `leis an mballa`, "Réamhfhocal + an: urú is gnáthaí". This contradicts the Ulster pattern and the app's own lesson "Réamhfhocal + an: séimhiú in Ulaidh".
3. **"bord" for table** appears in the core declension tables and three exercise lists; **"ag dúnadh"** (closing) appears in `IRREGULAR NOUNS.xls`. No "-amar/-eamar" and no "clois" in this scope.
4. **The 2005 declension set has real genitive errors** (for example `chun an háite`, `cois an sléibhe`, `dála an scoile`, `cóta mo mháthair`), so it needs correcting before any reuse.
5. **The app has no lessons at all on:** the article and gender, relative clauses, question words, demonstratives and `s'agam`, the vocative, the partitive genitive, the genitive after `chun/cois/dála/timpeall/trasna`, adjective plurals, counting people above ten, or the three irregular counting nouns (`bliain`, `uair`, `pingin`). The notes have ready-made content for every one of these.
6. **Incidental app finding:** the numbers lesson "Beirt, triúr, ceathrar" teaches `Beirt dhochtúirí`, while the Year 8 unit correctly has `beirt deartháireacha` (no lenition of d, t, s after `beirt`). One of the two is wrong; standard usage supports the Year 8 form, so `beirt dochtúirí` needs checking. Also, section `d2` holds the four first-declension genitive lessons while `d1` has only three plural lessons.

### 2b. Factual and spelling errors, by file

**Declension set (2005, "stu"/Cillian Breatnach).** These errors are in both the docs and the slides:
* 2nd: "Áit, chun an háite" should be `chun na háite`. "Sléibhte, cois an sléibhe" should be `cois an tsléibhe` (the headword should also be `sliabh`). "Scoil, dála an scoile" should be `dála na scoile`.
* 2nd: the weak-plural table gives "Cos, Cosa, Coise, **Cosa**" as the genitive plural, but two slides later it has `siúl na gcos`. The genitive plural is `cos`.
* 2nd: "Clan", "Brog", and "iolraí dár crích a" (for `dar críoch -a`).
* 3rd: "uirlís" should be `uirlis`, and "Tá na focal" should be `Tá na focail`. "Tá mé ag dul chun (Eoraip)" needs `na hEorpa` in the key.
* 4th: the heading "Ainmfhocail fhirinscneacha a chríochnaíonn ar ghuta" goes on to list "(B) aiste, fáilte, tréimhse, cuideachta". "fearradh na fáilte" should be `fearadh`. Two examples are not genitives at all: "cóta m'uncail" under "with the article", and "ag dul sa tseans".
* 5th: "Máthair, Cóta **mo mháthair**" should be `cóta mo mháthar`, which is the point of the slide. The rule says a consonant is added but gives "ceathrú, ceathrú"; the table later has `ceathrún`. Also "ag dol na cánach" (`díol`), "blath" (`bláth`), "olan" (`olann`), "ag déamhniú" (`deimhniú`), "ag treasnú" (`trasnú`).
* All five: "an iomarcha" should be `an iomarca`; "ag scúbadh" should be `ag scuabadh`.

**Teacher's `An Chéad Díochlaonadh.doc` (2008):**
* Typos: "Leathanch", "Abhar", "tuiseach ginideach", "doras an tseipéil" (`tséipéil`), "cáipín" (`caipín`).
* The answer for D.3 needs `ag fáil an phinsin`.

**XLS tables:**
* FIRST: "an dinnéar, **an dhinnéir**" should be `an dinnéir` (d is not lenited after `an`).
* FIFTH: "**nan** bhráthar" should be `an bhráthar`, and "na laethan**5**ta" should be `laethanta`. FIFTH also repeats the whole irregular list from `IRREGULAR NOUNS.xls`.
* SECOND: the meaning of `obair` is given as "bhe".
* THIRD: "an stráinséar" should be `strainséir`.
* IRREGULAR NOUNS: "ar aghaidh = in front of" should be "forward"; `os comhair` is "in front of".

**`An Tuiseal Ginideach.doc` (external):**
* The first table's columns are headed "Ainmfhocail Fhirinscneacha | Ainmfhocail Bhaininscneacha" but actually hold singular and plural.
* "Leis an máthar" should be `leis an máthair`.
* "ag seinnt" should be `ag seinm`.

**`An tAinmfhocal sa Ghaeilge 1.doc`, plural tables:**
* "múinteoir(í)" should be `múinteoirí`, and "cathaoireach" should be `cathaoireacha`.
* "caranna" should be `carranna`, "duaiseann" `duaiseanna`, "clusa" `cluasa`, and "bóithe" `bóithre`.
* "dlithe, croithe" should be `dlíthe, croíthe`. Also "commenest" and "The artice" (in `An tAlt ...`).

**`Úsáid an Ainmfhocail ...`:**
* "The are five classes of noun" should say five cases.
* "an léamh an pháipéir" should be `ag léamh`, "dala" should be `dála`, "an domhain, trasna an domhain" should start from `an domhan`, and "Tabhartach" should be `Tabharthach`.
* In the homework: "féinn", "Ba ghnach", and "ar an léachtaí" (`ar na léachtaí`).

**`Masculine or feminine.doc`:** "babóg" should be `bábóg`; "réanmhaisnéis" is a typo.

**`The Irish definite articles ...`:**
* It says "sl, sn, sr" take t, then lists "sc, **sl**, sm, sp, st" as unchanged.
* "na h-eaglaise" should be `na heaglaise`.

**Counting:**
* `Counting in Irish.doc` says "Beirt causes a shéimhiú on all nouns beginning with a consonant", but d, t and s after `beirt` resist it (`beirt deartháireacha`). See Headline 6: the same over-generalisation appears to be in the app's `uimhreacha` lesson.

## 4. Gap analysis against the app

The app's 30 sections have no section on the article, gender, cases, mutations as a whole, the vocative, relative clauses, question words, pronouns or demonstratives. Adjectives, numbers and adverbs share one area. Below, "new section" means a new grammar section; "new lesson" means an addition to an existing one.

### 4a. New sections the notes make easy

1. **Na míreanna ceisteacha (question words).** No app coverage. `Míreanna ceisteacha.doc` gives 14 Ulster forms with models:
   > "Cá háit | a ndeachaigh tú / a n-itheann tú lón"
   > "Cad chuige | a bhfuil brón ort?" ... "Cén dóigh | a ndearna tú é sin?"

   Possible lessons: `Cad é / Cé` (direct relative, `atá`); `Cá` + eclipsis (`Cá bhfuil`); `Cá háit / Cén áit` + indirect relative; `Cén t-am / Cén uair`; `Cad chuige / Cén fáth`; `Cá mhéad` + singular noun; `Cad é mar / Cén dóigh`. It links straight to the Comhrá track, where pupils must ask as well as answer.

2. **An clásal coibhneasta (relative clauses).** No app coverage. `RELATIVE.doc` supplies the rule grid ("DIRECT: A + Séimhiú ... INDIRECT: Ar + Séimhiú / A + Urú") and the adverbial list ("Áit (mar), Fáth (cúis, cuige, tuige), Dóigh (bealach, slí, caoi), Am"). The kuichinneide GIFs supply 34 ready-made paired sentences, ideal for a transform exercise:
   > "An fear a d'fhreagair an fón" / "An fear nár fhreagair an fón"
   > "Na bláthanna a fuair mé ó Sheán" / "Na bláthanna nach bhfuair mé ó Sheán"

   Possible lessons: direct relative (`a` + lenition, `d'` in the past, `a fuair`, `a dúirt`); negative direct (`nach` + eclipsis, `nár` in the past); indirect with `a` + eclipsis (`an teach a bhfuil mé i mo chónaí ann`); indirect past `ar`; after `an áit / an fáth / an dóigh / an t-am`.

3. **An tAlt agus inscne (article and gender).** No app coverage. From `An tAlt sa Ghaeilge 1.doc`: "an siopa ... an t-uisce", "an bhróg ... an tsráid ... an oíche", and the dentals rule. From `Masculine or feminine.doc`: "-óg, -eog ... -lann ... -acht (only nouns with more than one syllable)" are feminine; "-án, -ún, -ín, -óir, -éir" are masculine. `An tAinmfhocal sa Ghaeilge Obair Baile 1.doc` supplies 40 ready items for "add the article" and 20 for "identify the gender" (for example `doras, oileán, cailín, teach, uisce` and `tine, bean, farraige, bó, Gaeilge`). Remove `bord`.

4. **Na tuisil (cases) and the vocative.** No app coverage. `Úsáid an Ainmfhocail` defines each case with Ulster examples ("Dhruid Máire an doras agus d'oscail sí an fhuinneog"). The homework gives an "identify the case" task (13 sentences) and a "find the genitives" task (10 sentences). Vocative items: "A fhir! A ghirseach! A mhúinteoir! A bhanaltra! A chara!"

5. **Forainmneacha agus aidiachtaí taispeántacha (pronouns, demonstratives, `s'agam`).** No app coverage. From `An Forainm Pearsanta.doc`: "Buaileann sí gasúir" vs "Buaileann gasúir í". From IMU `An Aidiacht Taispeántach.doc`: "Chaill mé an cóta s'agam" becomes "Chaill mé mo chóta", and "Tá Pádraig s'againne thar a bheith láidir". This is authentic Ulster and uses the accepted `s'againne`.

### 4b. New lessons for existing sections

* **Genitive, `d1` to `d5` or a new "An ginideach: úsáidí eile" section.** The app teaches the genitive after nouns, after verbal nouns and (in `reamhfhocail-chomhshuite`) after compound prepositions. It never teaches:
  * the **partitive genitive**, from `Úsáid ... (f)`: "a lán airgid, an iomad oibre, an iomarca béime, beagán cainte, oiread céille, roinnt eolais";
  * the **simple prepositions that take the genitive**: "chun na scoile, cois na farraige, dála an scéil, timpeall na cathrach, trasna na sráide".

  Every declension file has worked examples of both (after correction).
* **`d1` restructure.** Move the four first-declension genitive lessons out of `d2` into `d1`, so `d2` covers only the second declension. `An Chéad Díochlaonadh.doc` (2008) adds lessons the app lacks: "Nouns containing -ia: an t-iasc, praghas an éisc" and **boys' names in the genitive**, exercise C ("Seán, Airgead Sheáin ... Breandán, Teach ...").
* **"Lagiolra nó tréaniolra?" (in `d1` or `d2`).** `Lagiolraí is Tréaniolraí.doc`: "bíonn lagiolra ag ainmfhocal má tá an ginideach iolra agus an t-ainmneach uatha mar an gcéanna" (capall, na capaill, na gcapall; bróg, na bróga, na mbróg) vs strong plurals (cóip, na cóipeanna, na gcóipeanna). This one test is what makes the genitive plural predictable. The app states the result piecemeal but never teaches the test.
* **"Cén díochlaonadh?"** The recognition table in `AN TUISEAL GINIDEACH 3.doc` (ending in nom sg, ending in gen sg, gender, examples: "1ú: Consan leathan, Consan caol, Firinscneach, Bád, báid ... 5ú: Guta nó consan caol, Consan leathan, Baininscneach (a bhformhór), Pearsa, pearsan; litir, litreach") would make an ideal intro lesson and a table on each `dN` screen.
* **Declension reference tables.** The XLS sheets give about 180 nouns in all four forms (for example "an chathair, na cathrach, na cathracha, na gcathracha"). Corrected, and with `bord` removed, they would make the app's per-section tables comprehensive and give a large item bank for drills. The source list comes from *Progress in Irish*, so it is a standard, well-chosen core vocabulary.
* **`d5`: place names.** From the 5th declension notes: "Roinnt ainmfhocal dílis atá an-choitianta: Éire, Alba, Ulaidh, Mumha" and from `An Tuiseal Ginideach.doc`: "Muintir na hAlban, Muintir na hÉireann". Also `chun na Fraince`, `Ollscoil na hÉireann` (Úsáid). There is currently no country or province genitive lesson.
* **`d4`: the diminutive `-ín` and nouns that never change.** `An Ceathrú ... doc` has "Tá an Ceathrú Díochlaonadh eisceachtúil go maith ... Cailín, ceant, club, téacs, tram, stop". These are good for a "loanwords are usually fourth declension" lesson.
* **`aidiachtai`: adjective plurals.** `An Aidiacht.doc`: "na fir mhóra, na fir chainteacha, na fir bheaga" and the task "na pinn mór, na cloig beag, na boscaí deas". The app teaches lenition after feminine singular only.
* **`aidiachtai`: "Is lú an dara madadh ná ..."** (`Céimeanna Comparáide`). The copula comparative frame is not in the app.
* **`uimhreacha`:**
  * **Irregular counters** (`Counting in Irish.doc`): "trí bliana, ceithre huaire, sé pingine, seacht mbliana". The app's "1 go 10" lesson omits `bliain` and `uair`, though Year 8 already uses `trí bliana déag`.
  * **People 11+** (`duine.gif`, `daichead.gif`): "aon duine dhéag, dháréag, trí dhuine dhéag, fiche duine, duine is fiche, céad duine, míle duine".
  * **Years**: "naoi déag ochtó", "sa bhliain dhá mhíle", "dhá mhíle is a cúig" (`UIMHREACHA`).
  * **Days**: `Dé Luain` vs `An Luan` (`deluan.gif` vs `UIMHREACHA`).
* **`copail`.** The app has classification, likes and `ba`. `An chopail .doc` adds:
  * **identification**: "Is é Seán an múinteoir", "Is í Nuala an duine is sine";
  * **ownership**: "Is liom an mála";
  * **emphasis or fronting**: "Is orm atá an locht", "Is ar maidin a tháinig sé";
  * **useful copula phrases**: "Is doiligh a rá", "Is minic a bhí mé thar lear", "Is iomaí uair a thit mé".

  These are high-value for AS speaking and writing. The IMU plan names the key contrast: "abairtí aicme v abairtí ionannais".
* **`reamhfhocail-simpli`: what prepositions mean.** `Prepositional Pronoun Samples.doc` lists the uses of each preposition ("AR: to wear, Tá hata uirthi; to have a name, Siobhán atá orm; having to do a thing, Tá orainn ...", "� - : desire or need, Tá deoch uaim", "FAOI: intention, Tá fúm ..."). After Ulsterising the mutations, that would make a strong idiom lesson.
* **`reamhfhocail-simpli`: `ar` without lenition.** `An tAlt, Réamhfhocail, etc.doc` and `An Séimhiú.doc` both explain `ar` + no lenition for states and times ("ar buile, ar ceal, ar meisce, ar maidin, ar ball") and `gan` ("gan pingin rua" vs "duine gan chiall"). This is AS-level precision the app does not have.
* **Mutation mnemonics as hints.** "Mary Got New Brogues Now Badly Dirtied" (`ANFHOI~1.DOC`); "many Boys go Camping near Ditches behind Fences nice Girls backpack down Town" and "SCALLIONS SMELL SPICY IN STEW" for sc, sm, sp, st (IMU). These suit the app's `hint` field.
