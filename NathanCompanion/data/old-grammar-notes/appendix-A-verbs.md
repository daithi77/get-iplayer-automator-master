# Old grammar notes: VERBS

Scope: every tense, conjugation, irregular verb, verbal noun and imperative file in `oldgram/txt/`, checked against the app's 17 verb sections (`r1/r2/nr` x five tenses, `ainm-briathartha`, `aidiacht-bhriathartha`, `reamhfhocail-briathra`), the Year 8 units (`units.json`) and the Comhrá AS topics (`comhra.json`). Read-only; nothing in the repo was changed.

Short verdict: the notes add little that the app's core tense sections do not already do better. The app's rules are cleaner and more Ulster than most of the notes. The value is in the **topics the app does not cover at all** (imperative, dependent clauses with gur/nár and go/nach, syncopated and one-syllable -igh verbs, negative questions), in **teaching devices** (mnemonics, the short/long x broad/slender grid, drill tables), and in **Year 8 and Comhrá material** (a past-tense daily-routine narrative, an "ag + verbal noun" sentence builder, classroom commands, a poem).

### 2b. Other non-Ulster forms

* Connacht/standard eclipsis after the article: "leis an bhfear", "leis an ngrian" (Past Samples, worksheet_2), "sa gcoill", "sa gcliabhán" (Future Samples), "sa ngort" (Br. Saor Samples), "sa bpairc" (Habitual Samples). Ulster: leis an fhear, sa choill, sa ghort, sa pháirc.
* Connacht words: "chuile", "uilig", "céard" (Daltaí; IRREGULAR VERBS.rtf also has "conas"). Ulster: achan, uilig is fine, cad é / caidé, cén dóigh.
* "chun na scoile" (worksheet_1, Cleachtadh 2), "chun fios a chur" (Future Samples); Ulster prefers "chuig an scoil" / "ar scoil" and "le".
* "tar éis" alongside "i ndiaidh" in the same worksheet (worksheet_1: "ar a leath uair tar éis a seacht" then "i ndiaidh a deich").
* Munster "dhein", "tigh" (Achoimre, cross-ref).
* Missing Ulster forms the notes could have used: gheibhim (faigh present), ní abraim / an abrann tú (abair dependent present). VERB ENDINGS does give "Tchím", "Tchífidh" and IRREGULAR VERBS.rtf gives "Bhearfaidh" (tabhair future); both good.

### 2c. Factual and spelling errors worth knowing (so nothing is copied across)

**Daltaí family**
* athraigh is labelled 1st conjugation in Past, Conditional, Habitual and Autonomous samples; it is 2nd.
* Present Tense: 2nd-conjugation ending given as "...(a)íann" (should be -(a)íonn). The root rule for -igh verbs says "accented vowel immediately after the igh" in three files and "before" in two.
* Present Samples: row E negatives read "Ní dhúisíonn sé..." (copied from row D); "Eiríonn" lacks its fada; "Nach ullmhaíonn" should be "Nach n-ullmhaíonn"; "i grcó" typo; "gach ré bliain" means every second year, glossed "every year"; "Eoin ... kills herself" (í fhéin for a man) here and in Past Samples.
* Habitual Samples: "Phósfadh daoine go hóg" is conditional (should be Phósadh); "le do srannach" (do shrannach); "don uaisle" (do na huaisle).
* Conditional Samples: "D'fágfaidís" (D'fhágfaidís); "Rithfeá ina dhiaidh na gasúir" (i ndiaidh na ngasúr).
* Future Samples: "na h-ainmhithe" (na hainmhithe); "i gcoinne an namhaid" (an namhad); "dena cairde".
* Br. Saor: the 2nd-conjugation table has shifted cells: present broad shows "-ófar", future broad shows "-ódh", so the whole column is wrong. Samples: "Codlaíodh" with negatives "Níor chodlódh / Ar codlódh / Nár gcodlódh" (should be Níor/Ar/Nár codlaíodh); "Nífí siad", "Sheastaí siad" (autonomous takes iad); S2 "Sroichfí" is conditional but labelled habitual (and should be Shroichfí); T "Taispeánadh" is past but labelled habitual; U "Ullmhaítear" is present, glossed "was prepared", labelled habitual; "fógartha" (fógra).

**Teacher's worksheets**
* "Má tá an briathar caol, 'i,o'" (An Aimsir Láithreach 2, An Aimsir Fháistineach): should be i, e.
* "Suí (sit)" given as a root in five files; the root is Suigh. Leathanach Oibre then has "Ar shuí sibh?" and "Nár shuí tú?" (shuigh).
* "ag deireadh an bhriathar" (an bhriathair).
* An Dara Réimniú: ending table "aímid muid" doubles the subject; Inis and Imigh sit in a list headed "get rid of the aigh".
* An Aimsir Fháistineac1 is the past-tense cloze under a future title: its word bank has "Chuaigh", its fixed text has "Bhí an lá go deas" and "níor (ith)".
* Leathanach Oibre: "Nár nigh tú do aghaidh?" (d'aghaidh), "an imreoir" (an t-imreoir), "an bruscair" (an bruscar), "na bhaile" (abhaile), "sbh", two items lettered D.
* Na Briathra Neamhrialt_Trí_aimsir: "Ní Bhéirfaidh" (Ní bhéarfaidh), "Ní Dheirim" (Ní deirim), "Ní Dheirfidh" (Ní déarfaidh), "Ní Déanaim" (Ní dhéanaim), random capitals. Na Briathra Neamhrialta: "thugeann", "fhaighaimid", "Ní déanaim/déanann", "Ní dheirim/dheireann".
* worksheet_1: "Muscail", "chodhladh", "na bhaile". worksheet_2: "Cuideamh de dhith le na briathra" (Cuidiú de dhíth leis na briathra); "Níor nigh mé m'aghaidh (I did not clean my face)"; "Nár rith tú na bhaile?".
* An aimsir chaite 2: "'R': Rith mé = Run!" (Rith! = Run!).
* cass: "I catch: Rug mé"; "Chonaic me"; "(dean: muid)".
* Cleachtadh 2: "mo cheirteach" (ceirt is a rag; use mo chuid éadaigh); "briarthra".
* PPT: "An seachtain seo caite" (An tseachtain), "RIALL" (Riail), "Yesteday"; lists "Infinitives" which are imperatives; ith is missing from the 10 irregulars.
* An modh ordaitheach: plurals "tógaígí", "seasáigí", "stopaígí" (tógaigí, seasaigí, stopaigí); "siúl" (siúil); "buail/rith/bris" and "fágaigí/fanaigí" in the negative table lack Ná.
* VERB ENDINGS: "ÉIRIGH = Éirim" (Éirím). DAOINE FÁSTA: "an mBriseann", "an mBailíonn" (an mbriseann), "Bailionn".
* IRREGULAR VERBS.rtf: "Níor itheann sé" (Ní itheann), "Ní Choiseann", "dependant".

## 3. Arrangement: proposed teaching order and file map

Principle: root first (the imperative is the root), then regular before irregular, 1st conjugation before 2nd, and a tense's positive before its question and negative. Two changes from the app's current order are suggested: teach the **imperative** first, and teach the **conditional before the past habitual**, because the habitual endings are the conditional endings without the f (chuirfinn / chuirinn, chuirfeá / chuirteá, chuirfidís / chuiridís). That makes the habitual a quick "take out the f" lesson.

## 4. Gap analysis against the app

The app has 17 verb sections and about 190 walk-through lessons across five tenses x three verb groups, plus verbal noun, verbal adjective and verbs with prepositions. Its rules are sound and Ulster (tábla, achan, chuig, tchím, analytic muid, both conditional plurals). Against that, the notes offer the following.

### 4a. Topics the app does not teach at all

1. **The imperative (an modh ordaitheach).** No section; "Ná" appears only incidentally. Notes: An modh ordaitheach (sg/pl table, "Ná héist / Ná hól", 21 sentences such as "Don't you (pl) run in the corridor"); Copy of Infinitives ("Oscail an fhuinneog" then "D'iarr tú orm an fhuinneog a oscailt"); the irregular imperatives ("Téigh! Gabh!", "Beir ar!"). A section could run: 2sg root; 2pl -aigí/-igí (-aígí/-ígí for 2nd conj); Ná + h before a vowel; irregulars (tar, gabh/téigh, abair, tabhair, bí); 1st plural -aimis ("Imrímis"); reported commands with "a + VN" (links to ainm-briathartha "Rud a dhéanamh").
2. **Dependent clauses: gur/nár (past) and go/nach + urú (other tenses).** gur appears only in the copula section and Comhrá; the verb tenses never practise "Tá a fhios agam gur bhris sé" or "Deir sé go n-imríonn sé". Notes: cass drill ("I know that he closed the window: Tá a fhios agam gur dhruid sé an fhuinneog"; "...nár dhruid sé..."); IRREGULAR VERBS.rtf ("Some irregular verbs have special dependant forms which have to be used after: an, ní, nach, cá, go, mura ... Study the following words, as they do not change the verb: Cé, cad, ... nuair, ... má"); cross-ref Gramadach 1 ("Cuir Deir sé roimh gach abairt"; "Dúirt sé leat dá suífeá síos go ciúin nach mbrisfeá an chathaoir"). This is core AS writing and Comhrá language ("Deir mo dhaidí gur...", "Sílim go mbeidh...").
3. **Syncopated 2nd-conjugation verbs** (oscail, imir, inis, labhair, codail, múscail, freagair, ceangail, cosain, díbir). The app's r2 sections use only -aigh/-igh verbs; osclaíonn, imríonn, inseoidh, labhróidh, músclaím do not appear in any tense lesson. These are among the verbs pupils use most (imir, inis, oscail). Notes: An Dara Réimniú ("Exceptions: Múscail, Mhúscail, Músclaím. Oscail, D'oscail, Osclaím ... Labhair, Labhair, Labhraím"); An Aimsir Fháistineac_dara_réimniú ("Bí cúramach: Ceangail, Ceanglóidh; Oscail, Osclóidh; Imir, Imreoidh; Inis, Inseoidh; Cosain, Cosnóidh; Díbir, Díbreoidh"); the Daltaí explanation of syncopation is the clearest wording ("squeeze the vowels out of the last syllable ... codail ... codl-"), worth paraphrasing, not copying.
4. **One-syllable -igh verbs** (nigh, léigh, suigh, dóigh, luigh, pléigh, buaigh). Not taught; they break the -igh = 2nd conjugation rule pupils learn. Notes: An Aimsir Láithreach 2 ("Dóigh: Dóim; Nigh: Ním; Léigh: Léim; Suigh: Suím") and An Aimsir Fháistineach ("Dófaidh, Nífidh, Léifidh, Suífidh"). A nice trap item: "Léim" is both "I read" and "jump".
5. **Negative questions with Nár and Nach.** "Nár" never appears in the verb lessons. Notes: Past Tense (Daltaí) "Nár: Negative Questions"; worksheet_2 ("Nár ghlan tú an teach?", "Nár ól tú an tae?"); every Daltaí sample shows Nach + urú in the other tenses. Useful for Comhrá tag questions and persuasive writing.

### 4b. Better explanations, mnemonics, tables

6. **Mnemonics** (An Aimsir Chaite.doc): "Barbie caught dopey friends going mad publicly, silly twits" (b c d f g m p s t take h); "he likes noodles and rice" (h l n r do not); "scallions smell spicy in stew" (sc sm sp st do not; also in An aimsir chaite 2). worksheet_2 tags examples "Little Nicky", "Vowels", "'F'". The app's hints are plain rules ("Consonant verb: add h after the first letter"). Note the app's rule lists l, n, r, sc, sp, st; the mnemonic also covers sm, which the app mentions in the present but not in r1-chaite.
7. **The 2x2 ending grid** (VERB ENDINGS; DAOINE FÁSTA): rows slender/broad, columns short (one syllable)/long (two syllables), one grid per tense and person, e.g. future "Slender: -fidh / -eoidh; Broad: -faidh / -óidh", with one example verb per cell (Bris, Tóg, Éirigh, Ceannaigh). The app teaches each cell in a separate section and never shows the whole grid; a reference card at the start of each tense group would tie the r1/r2 sections together.
8. **"Group A / Group B" framing of past irregulars** (An Aimsir Chaite.doc: "In the negative with Group A we still use 'Níor' ... This time, we use 'Ní' in the negative and 'An' in the question"). The app already splits nr-chaite this way; the labels and the one-line summary would make a good hint.
9. **Ulster variants** to mention as accepted alternatives: "Tchífidh" (VERB ENDINGS), "Bhearfaidh mé" for tabhair (IRREGULAR VERBS.rtf), "Ar dhúirt tú? / Níor dhúirt mé" and "You will also hear Níor dhúirt" (The irregular verbs in the past tense; Trí aimsir), "Gabh!" as the imperative of téigh. None appear in the app (it has Tchím only). gheibh is on the teacher's accepted list but in neither the notes nor the app.
10. **Verbal nouns grouped by pattern** (COMMON VERBAL NOUNS runs roughly -adh/-eadh, then -ú, then -í, then -t/-int, then -áil, then irregulars) and the three-column Verb | VN | VA table (cross-ref Gramadach 2: "Tar | Ag teacht | Tagtha"). The app's "an t-ainm briathartha ceart" lesson gives examples but no pattern table.

### 4c. Exercise types the app lacks

The app's item shapes are stem, transform, question, free and gap. The notes use:

* **Matching** (Meaitseáil: 32 roots to English); **flashcards** (Caitríona: root on one side, "Músclaím" on the other).
* **Passage-level cloze with a word bank** (worksheet_1: "(Múscail) ____ mé ar a seacht a chlog ... (Ith) ____ mé ____ ____ agus (ól) ____ mé ____ tae", word bank "arán rósta, bhricfeasta, staighre..."); and cass "Mar a chaith mé an lá inné" with person cues "(Imir: muid) peil sa pháirc ... (ní: ceannaigh: muid) milseáin".
* **Rewrite a whole passage** in another tense, then in the negative (Cleachtadh 2: "Athscríobh an sliocht seo agus cuir na briathra san aimsir chaite. Déan arís é san fhoirm dhiúltach").
* **The 7-row transformation drill** (cass): positive, negative, question, yes, no, "I know that...", "I know that ... not", for one verb at a time.
* **English to Irish translation** (ceacht-caite: "I woke up at half past seven", "I played camógaíocht with my friends at five past seven"; Leathanach Oibre: 26 Irish sentences to English).
* **Poem** (dán-briathra neamhrialta: "D'ith mé mo shuipéar agus d'ól mé mo thae / Bhí mise sásta ag deireadh an lae").

### 4d. For the Year 8 track

* **New sentence builder "Cad atá ar siúl?"** from an t-ainm briathartha: rows "Tá / Níl / An bhfuil" + "an fear, an bhean, an déagóir, an gasúr, an páiste..." + "ag rith, ag snámh, ag ithe, ag caint, ag ceol, ag súgradh" + "sa chistin, sa pháirc, sa ghairdín, san uisce". Exactly the app's builder format; the notes also model the question "Cé atá ag rith?".
* **Classroom commands unit** from Copy of Infinitives ("Glan an clár dubh", "Scríobh ar an chlár dubh", "Éist leis an mhúinteoir", with druid replacing dún).
* **"An lá inné" (yesterday) unit** to follow b7 "Mo lá" (present): ceacht-caite sentences, Briathra san aimsir chaite, briathra ceacht1 ("(Níor: múscail: Seán) ar leath i ndiaidh a seacht").
* **The poem** as an audio track with a gap-fill on the irregular verbs.

### 4e. For the Comhrá AS track

* A **past-tense narrative** model answer ("Cad é a rinne tú inné / an deireadh seachtaine seo caite?") built from cass "Mar a chaith mé an lá inné", covering both irregular groups and the analytic plural (d'imir muid, d'ith muid). The existing as-deireadh-seachtaine topic is present habitual only.
* **Conditional prompts**: "Caidé a dhéanfá dá mbainfeá an Crannchur Náisiúnta?" and "Caidé a dhéanfá dá mbeadh ort a bheith i mbun an tí?" (cross-ref Gramadach 1).
* **Reported opinion**: gur/nár and go/nach frames ("Tá a fhios agam gur...", "Deir sé nach...") from the dependent-clause material above.

## 6. Cautions before reusing anything

* **Copyright:** the Daltaí na Gaeilge files (13), IRREGULAR VERBS.rtf ("Cillian Walsh ©") and Glancecard.pdf are third-party. Use them for ideas only; write fresh examples.
* **Do not import the Daltaí sample tables or Br. Saor**: the error rate is too high and the dialect is Connacht/standard.
* **Replace Dún with druid** wherever a worksheet is adapted, and pick a new broad model verb (tóg or glan) because druid is slender.
* **Remove -amar** from cass and lead with the analytic plural in all paradigm cards.
* Bailigh, Ceannaigh and Copy of Infinitives show doubled vowels in the text conversion ("Bailíím", "túú"); check the originals before quoting.
