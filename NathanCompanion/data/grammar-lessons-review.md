- Place name: "Dún na nGall" became "Tír Chonaill" in two items (nr-laithreach, r2). Restore "Dún na nGall" if the ruling is about the verb only.
- "pizza" became "píotsa"; "don phríomhoide" became "don mhúinteoir".
- Added practice items in r2 (-igh to -eoidh): "(Deisigh) sé an ríomhaire glúine amárach", "(Sínigh) sí an cárta lá breithe".
- New sentences to check: "An féidir leat an fhuinneog a dhruidim?"; "Meicneoir: Bíonn sé/sí ag deisiú carranna."; "Tchí sé an bus ag teacht. Ní fheiceann sé…"; "Rachfar chun na Gaeltachta sa samhradh."; "Feictear an fharraige ón bhus."; "Tá an siopa ar oscailt ar a naoi a chlog."

- r1 verbal noun lists kept as they were (dún row removed); not every form re-checked.

## copail, sealbhach, uimhreacha, dobhriathar, aidiachtai
- Place name: "Dún na nGall" became "Gaoth Dobhair" in two sealbhach sentences and one uimhreacha sentence (the other agent used "Tír Chonaill"). To be made consistent once you decide.
- dún replaced: "Dhún mé mo chóipleabhar" became "Dhruid mé mo chóipleabhar".
- dobhriathar: new sentences "Tá an cat thuas ar bharr an bhalla."; "Tá na gasúir ag teacht aníos ón trá."; "Chuaigh an rang síos go bun an chnoic."; a model sentence now uses "mar" instead of "nó"; tables gained short English glosses.
- aidiachtai: new item "Níl an traein chomh (callánach) leis an bhus scoile."; the lesson teaches h before a vowel after chomh ("chomh hard"). Gap items now show the plain adjective in brackets. Item "gurb é an duine is fearúla ar an fhoireann é" gained its closing "é".
- New worked examples: "níos mó", "is breátha", "Ceathrar múinteoirí", "An t-ochtú rang", "Beirt chailíní", "B'álainn an lá é".
- Dropped (no practice items): copail Is ea / Ní hea, Sin é/í/iad, plain "Is maith le Peadar"; aidiachtai broad comparatives (ard, airde) and vowel-dropping ones (ramhar, raimhre), still in the tables; definition-matching drills.
- uimhreacha: labels above 20 dropped ("Caibidil a fiche a cúig", "Bus a seasca a trí") because the agent was unsure of the "a" before the ten. Please confirm the right form.
- Small lessons: sealbhach "M' agus d' roimh ghuta" (3 items); dobhriathar "Isteach, istigh, amach, amuigh" (5 items).

## r1-gnathchaite, r2-gnathchaite, nr-gnathchaite, r1-coinniollach, r2-coinniollach
- dún replaced: glan as the broad model verb in r1-gnathchaite and r1-coinniollach tables ("(Glan) sé an siopa…"); croch also used; druid in "Dá mbeadh fuinneog mo sheomra ar oscailt, dhruidfinn í" and "Dhruidfí na siopaí".
- Place name: "Dún na nGall" became "Tír Chonaill" (2 items).
- Negative answers in r1-coinniollach are now the negative only ("Ní íocfaidís."); free answers are the reply only ("Dhéanfainn staidéar.").
- Small fixes: "mí Mheán Fómhair"; "ar an aip" became "ar líne"; commas after dá clauses.
- New sentences to check: "Bhínn i gcónaí tuirseach ar an Luan"; "Thagaidís chuig an teach achan Domhnach"; "Dhéantá an obair bhaile sa leabharlann"; "Cheannaínn greannán achan seachtain"; "Dhúisíodh mo mháthair mé…"; "Chuirfeadh sí cóta fearthainne uirthi"; "Cad é a dhéanfá dá mbeadh tart ort?".
- Third person plural differs: r2-coinniollach uses the analytic "cheannódh siad", while r1-coinniollach and the r2-gnathchaite table use -óidís / -faidís. Which do you want taught?
- Doubtful sentences kept: "Bhítí ag súil leis an bhus ag a hocht a chlog"; "Thagtaí ar fhreagra sa leabharlann…"; "Bhainteá an cluiche i gcónaí" (model question "An mbaininn…?"); "Sháródh sé an fhadhb…"; "Cheansódh an gasúr an capall dá mbéarfadh sé air".
- "Dhéanfaí" (déan) sits in the regular broad saorbhriathar list.

## d1 to d5 (declensions)
- dún replaced: "Dúnfaidh mé" became "Druidfidh mé"; "Ag dúnadh" became "Ag druidim" (would "ag druid" be more natural?); "Dhún siad" became "Dhruid siad".

- d1 has 3 lessons (plurals only); first-declension genitive is taught in d2.
- d4: "Guth an scéalaí" and "Guth an amhránaí" became plural items ("Na scéalaithe", "Na hamhránaithe").
- d4 -anna rule ("vowel ending: add -nna"); "gé, géanna" dropped as it doesn't fit.
- To check: "Doras na bpaisinéirí" (could read as "the passengers' door"); "Tháinig mo chuid cairde" (no séimhiú on cairde after chuid).
- "an príomhoide" kept in two d3 sentences as ordinary school vocabulary.
- Left as they were: d5 table "Uncail na máithreacha" (reads oddly); d4 "An oíche (b)", "An teanga (b)" with gender markers.
