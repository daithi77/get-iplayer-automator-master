# Mark2011 booklets: what they offer Gan Ainm

## Summary

The zip holds five Easter-course (Cúrsa na Cásca) workbooks from Coláiste Mhuire Loch an Iúir, dated 2006 to 2010, plus three covers. The PDF metadata credits Dáithí, but most of the content is not his alone. He typeset the AS, A2-1 and A2-2 booklets in Pages. The credit pages say AS and A2-1 were compiled by the Loch an Iúir education subcommittee, of which he was a member. The A2 translation booklet (A2-2) is Pádraig de Bléine's work, printed "le caoinchéad". The reading and grammar booklets are scans of older third-party material, including the Anois and An tUltach newspapers and Peadar Mac Muiris's diploma sheets. None of the booklets has an answer key.

The most valuable things to take are the **exercise formats**, which cost nothing in rights:
- gap fill with a word bank that includes distractors;
- a table matching sentence beginnings to endings, with one spare ending;
- a synonym hunt ("Aimsigh san alt"), with core items and "Obair Bhreise";
- paraphrase ("Cuir Gaeilge eile");
- translation passages cut into five chunks worth [10] each.

These formats map straight onto a reading and translation mode. The committee-written passages on alcohol and on smoking (smoking rewritten as vaping), the two environment texts and the page of 18 opinion frames can be used after correction. Beyond those:
- About 25 grammar lists feed specific existing lessons.
- Four new lessons are clearly needed: direct and indirect relative clauses, Má/Dá/Mura, copula classification and identification, and Tá versus Bíonn.
- The booklets support an A2 track in six units.

Blockers:
- The third-party journalism cannot be reused as it stands.
- Every listening item comes from exam papers and names the board.
- The content dates from 2009 to 2011.
- There are about 60 errors, some Munster and Connacht forms, and eight synthetic first-person plural forms.
- Several items raise safeguarding concerns: a suicide essay prompt, the Ó Searcaigh passages, and child-abuse statistics.

### 2c. Breaches of the house rulings

- **Synthetic first-person plural (present and future):**
  - feicfimid (Éamonn Ó Catháin passage);
  - Ceannaímid and gcuirimid, the latter in the word bank (A2-1 gap fill);
  - mairimid (A2-1 An Timpeallacht);
  - Bímid (Bart Simpson);
  - rachaimid ×2 and Fanaimid (A2-3 Má/Dá exercise).

  Use feicfidh muid, ceannaíonn muid, a maireann muid, and so on.
- **"clois":** "Cloisfear agallaimh": "Cluinfear".
- **"bord" meaning a table:** "6 (bord)", "11 (bord)", "na bord donn", and "BORD" in the plural table of A2-5. Use "tábla". The AS "ar bord" (aboard a ship) is correct.
- **"dún" and "-amar":** none found.
- **"students":** the English source texts say "students" about 15 times (Gaeltacht, University life, Independent Learner, A2-5), where the house rule is "pupils".
- **The exam board is named, with paper references:** on every listening item, on the "Cluas, Traic 22/27" credits, and in the A2-1 "Sa mhodúl A2:1" heading. Assessment-objective mark tallies appear throughout AS and A2-1. Strip all of it.

### 2d. Dated content (2009 to 2011 facts)

- The Celtic Tiger appears throughout, including "Cad é atá i ndán don Tiogar Ceilteach?" and "Now days ... the Celtic Tiger".
- Micheál Máirtín is named as Minister for Health in the Toitíní passage. The smoking bans that passage anticipates have been law since 2004 and 2007.
- The 11+ is "to end in 2004" (A2-2), and Caitríona Ruane is announcing the end of the 11+ (A2-1).
- Android "from mid-2008", MySpace, Google's 18-month data retention.
- SARS 2003: calls the WHO the "World Health Association" and says "Para-Olympics", but the athletes turned away were Special Olympics athletes.
- The Bam earthquake (Iran, 2003) and a Manchester tremor.
- The first rugby at Croke Park in 2007.
- The Navan Fort centre closing and reopening.
- Neil Lennon.
- Age Concern and Help the Aged, now merged.
- Dublin Corporation, now renamed.
- "Mobile phones came on the market a few years ago", with the claim that masts cause cancer (misinformation).
- The TG4 listing of Thursday 19 February (2009).
- Pól Ó Muirí's job title, the Pacific Dawn, and Ernesto Samper (president 1994 to 1998).
- Relative dates that have gone stale, such as "fiche bliain ó shin" since Chernobyl, now 40 years.
- Van Morrison's "latest album" (1997).
- "£" used for the Republic in A2-2.

### 2e. Content unsuitable for a school app in 2026

- **Cathal Ó Searcaigh:** two passages (A2-2 p. 57, A2-3). He has been the subject of serious allegations since 2008. Exclude.
- **Suicide essay prompt** "Sláinte Mheabhrach & Fadhb an Fhéinmharaithe" (AS p. 35). Keep it out of self-study mode, or reframe it with signposting to support services.
- **McMillen article** (A2-3): includes child rape statistics and advice about where it is unsafe to speak Irish.
- **An Triail summary:** mentions a teacher's sexual relationship with a pupil, infanticide and suicide. Use only if the play is on the course, and teacher-led.
- **Opinion-frame examples to replace:**
  - a refugee example ("deireadh leis na teifigh teacht isteach");
  - a pro-smoking example ("tugann siad faoiseamh dom");
  - "múinteoirí falsa".
- **A2-2 content:**
  - "Catholics play Gaelic football ... Protestants play soccer";

### 3a. Reading and translation mode

**Question types the booklets use, and how to build them.** The app's current item shapes are free, gap, question, stem and transform.

| Booklet type | Build as |
|---|---|
| Aimsigh san alt focal atá ar comhchiall le... (5 core + 7 "Obair Bhreise") | New **find** shape: tap the phrase in the passage |
| Tús agus críoch na habairte (10 beginnings, 11 endings, one spare) | New **match** shape |
| Gap fill from a word bank (10 gaps and 15 words, or 14 gaps and 20 words; each word used once) | `gap` plus a `bank` array that allows distractors |
| Cuir Gaeilge eile / Abair ar dhóigh eile | `free` with a model answer, `self: true` |
| Críochnaigh na habairtí i do chuid focal féin | `free` |
| Questions in Irish, marks [1] to [4], "caillfear marcanna ar fhreagraí a bhainfear focal ar fhocal" | `question` with key points and a "don't lift" check (flag answers that copy seven or more words from the text) |
| An síleann tú...? Bíodh fianaise agat | `free` + evidence |
| Meaitseáil focal agus ciall | `match` |
| Translation in [10] chunks | New **chunk** type: one chunk per screen, model answer, self-mark against 3 to 5 key structures |

**Passages to use**

- **Ship after correction, with the college's consent:**
  - AS **Alcól** (about 380 words): text, 14-gap bank, 11 Cuir Gaeilge eile items. Add 5 questions.
  - AS **Toitíní**, rewritten as **Na toitíní leictreonacha** with all four exercises kept.
  - AS **Taisteal agus turasóireacht** (translate into English, 6 paragraphs, still current).
  - A2-1 **An timpeallacht á scrios againn?** (gap fill).
  - A2-1 **An Timpeallacht**, updated, with its 10/11 matching and 5 questions.
- **Rewrite in the same register:** Turasóireacht na Gaeilge, An Teaghlach Nua-aimseartha, and the fatherhood column (keep "an tuarastal a chur ar an tábla").

Draft opening for the vaping text, for sign-off:

> Is cosúil go bhfuil na toitíní leictreonacha i ngach áit sa lá atá inniu ann. Síleann cuid mhór daoine óga go bhfuil siad slán, nó níos sábháilte ar scor ar bith ná na toitíní. Ach tá nicitín iontu, agus is druga é an nicitín. Is gairid go mbíonn an t-úsáideoir gafa leis. Bíonn blasanna milse orthu agus dathanna geala ar na paicéid, rud a mheallann an t-aos óg. Deir dochtúirí nach bhfuil a fhios againn go fóill cén dochar a dhéanfaidh siad do na scamhóga san fhadtéarma.

Sample items for that draft:
- **Find:** sábháilte → *slán*; tarraingíonn → *a mheallann*.
- **Match:** "Síleann cuid mhór daoine óga" … "go bhfuil siad níos sábháilte ná na toitíní".
- **Question:** *Cad chuige a síleann daoine óga go bhfuil na toitíní leictreonacha slán? [2]*

**Translation into Irish.** Use A2-2's structure: 5 chunks of [10]. The best candidates to redo or rewrite are:
- Elderly, Irish-medium education, Gaeltacht, Travel, Holidays;
- Radio (update to Raidió Fáilte and podcasts), Diet, Road safety, the three Environment passages;
- Disability, Sport and referees, Navan Centre;
- the four grammar passages.

Drop: SARS, the earthquakes, Unemployment and the Celtic Tiger, the 11+, University, Croke Park, the Irish League, Racism (rewrite it), Democracy, Vandalism, Violence, Ó Searcaigh.

Sample model answers:
- Gaeltacht: "Every year thousands of pupils go to the Gaeltacht to improve their Irish." → *Gach bliain téann na mílte dalta chun na Gaeltachta le feabhas a chur ar a gcuid Gaeilge.* / "They hear native speakers speaking Irish and they learn new words and phrases." → *Cluineann siad cainteoirí dúchais ag labhairt Gaeilge agus foghlaimíonn siad focail agus nathanna úra.*
- Autonomous form: → *Briseadh isteach i scoil in Iarthar Bhéal Feirste ag an deireadh seachtaine. Briseadh táblaí, soilse agus fuinneoga. Cruthófar cuid mhór post.*
- Conditional: → *Dá mbainfinn milliún punt, thaistealóinn an domhan mór. Cheannóinn teach úr níos mó do mo mháthair fosta. D'athródh sí teach amárach dá bhfaigheadh sí an seans.*
- Copula: → *Is meicneoir é Seán. Deir daoine gurb é an meicneoir is fearr sa cheantar é. Is í Síle bean chéile Sheáin.*
- Genitive: → *Tá Seán ina chónaí os comhair na scoile. Tá an scoil suite ar bharr an chnoic. Tá cuid mhór talaimh ag deartháir a mháthar.*

## 4. Page-layout conventions (the teacher's canonical style)

**AS and A2-1 (his typesetting, in Pages)**

*Page and type*
- Helvetica throughout.
- Page number bottom centre on A4 pages, bottom outer corner on the A5 spreads.

*Hierarchy, top to bottom*
- **Section label** in bold capitals, top left: LÉAMH, AISTRIÚCHÁN, ÉISTEACHT, SCRÍOBH.
- **Bold instruction** in sentence case beneath it: "Léigh an t-alt thíos agus freagair na ceisteanna a leanann." / "Aistrigh go Béarla. Ná haistrigh an teideal." / "Freagair i nGaeilge. Scríobh 200-250 focal."
- **Passage title** bold and centred.

*Passages and credits*
- Translation passages have wide gaps between paragraphs, to mark the chunks.
- Source credit set right: "© arna chóiriú as ...".

*Questions and marks*
- Question labels in bold: (a), (i).
- Marks in square brackets, set right: [1], [2].
- A key word in bold: "Tabhair **dhá** phointe".
- Full-width answer rules.
- Group totals in bold at the foot. Drop the assessment-objective labels.

*Box styles*
- Essay stimulus in a dotted-border box with a photograph inset and justified text, followed by bulleted discussion points.
- Gap fills double-spaced, with a bordered word-bank table of bold words in four or five columns.
- Matching as a ruled table headed "Tús na habairte | Críoch na habairte", with a roman-numeral column and an empty answer box, then the endings (a) to (k), each starting with "...".
- Opinion frames numbered, each with an italic bulleted example.

*Front matter and covers*
- Credit page: compilers' names, "Cúrsa na Cásca [year]".
- A2-2 adds "Go mbaine sibh tairbhe as."
- Covers: the college logo; "Cúrsa na Cásca"; a title in a coloured band or uncial lettering; a stock photograph.

**A2-2**
- A4 portrait, a bold centred English title, paragraphs each ending "[10]", no answer space.

**Loch an Iúir scanned house style (2006 to 2009)**
- A black vertical spine band with the level in white serif capitals ("A2/3", "GCSE").
- A large serif title ("Léamh").
- A dashed box with "Ainm: ___ Rang: ___".
- Inside: Times, bordered exercise boxes, numbered exercise groups (1. Meaitseáil, 2. Abair ar dhóigh eile, 3. grammar, 4. Freagair), and two-column masculine and feminine tables.

**Modernising for 2026**
- **Keep:** the capital section label with a bold instruction, the centred title, bracketed marks, the [10] chunk markers, the word-bank table and the two-column matching table.
- **Drop:** the booklet imposition, the stock photographs, the logo, the exam-board and assessment-objective text, and the Name/Class box (the app knows the pupil).
- **Add:** answer keys and Ulster-checked model answers throughout.
