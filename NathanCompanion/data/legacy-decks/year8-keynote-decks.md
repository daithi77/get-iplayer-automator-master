# Year 8 Keynote decks (2012 to 2013) and how they could feed Gan Ainm

## Summary

I went through all 14 decks in scope: 311 slides in total, 60 of them in a duplicate deck. They are short picture decks with one idea per slide. They are mostly in Ulster Irish (Cad é mar, Cad chuige, madadh, sceadamán, tiontaigh, gabh amach, druid) and have "Dul siar" review slides. The two number decks and Mé Féin use builds to hold back the answer, which is good retrieval practice. Mé Féin Part 2 is the strongest: it has model paragraphs and a "Feabhas...?" (improve this) task.

**Errors a pupil should not copy:**
- 19 is written "a haon déag" twice, and one slide pairs 19 with "a deich".
- "airgead" is used as a colour.
- "Ta" is missing its fada.
- "Obair Baile" should be "Obair bhaile".
- "trí - cheathrú" has an en dash and is not idiomatic as a time.
- "a dó déag" should be "a dó dhéag".
- "Cuir na lámha suas" mixes a singular verb with plural hands.
- "Lios Iomana" is spelt inconsistently.
- The model texts use dashes.

**Best number deck:** Uimhreacha-0-19-updated. It is identical to Uimhreacha_0-19 except that the stray "Dathanna" closing slide has been fixed, but it still has the three errors for 19. The deck called "Uimhreacha" is really a telling-the-time deck.

**Gaps in the app's Year 8 track:** greetings, "Cad é mar atá tú?", classroom language, numbers 0 to 100, colours, body parts, question words, clock time beyond o'clock and half past, and bare month names.

**Top recommendations:**
1. Fix "sa Sruthán" in the app's b1 and b4 to "sa tSruthán".
2. Add a new unit "Beannachtaí" (greetings and feelings).
3. Add classroom language: a pupil unit plus a command card for teachers.
4. Make small additions to b1, b2, b3, b5 and b7.
5. Build a numbers unit from tens and units chunks.
6. Add colours and body-part units, including a monster builder.

**Images:** nearly all are web clipart, stock photos or copyrighted characters (The Simpsons, Obama, Ed Sheeran, Beckham). Do not reuse them. Only the school crest, the photo of the school building and two digit screenshots look like the school's own.

**Patterns worth keeping in the app:**
- A "Bliain 8" title slide on each deck.
- One item per slide, glossed by a picture rather than English.
- A build reveal (numeral, then the Irish), which is the app's recall step.

**Rulings check:**
- No "dún" verb. "Dún Dealgan" is a place name, so it is fine.
- No "-amar/-eamar", no "clois", no "bord", no exam board, no "student".
- Dash breaches: the en dash in "trí - cheathrú" and the spaced hyphens noted above.

**Related problems in the app (outside the decks, found while comparing):**
- **"sa Sruthán" in b1 and b4 should be "sa tSruthán".** An "s" followed by a vowel, l, n or r takes a "t" after "sa", as in "sa tsiopa".
- web/data/vocab.json item G0625 reads "druid /dún", which breaches the "dún" ruling.
- Group names in vocab.json use en dashes ("Uimhreacha  -  orduimhreacha...", "An t-am  -  an clog...").

## 3. How the decks compare with the Year 8 units

**Where they overlap:**
- **b1 Mé féin and Mé Féin 1:** name, age and where you live match. The app uses "Cad é an aois thú?"; the deck uses "Cén aois thú?". Recall should accept both. The deck also has "Cé thusa?" and teaches eclipsis with the mnemonic; the app only shows eclipsis through local place names.
- **b2 Teaghlach and Mé Féin 2:** "Tá cúigear i mo theaghlach" and the pets match (the app has "Tá madadh againn fosta"). The app lacks the question "An bhfuil peata agat?" and a negative answer. The deck's "Cá mhéad duine atá i do theaghlach?" is more personal than the app's "...atá sa teaghlach?".
- **b3 Cur síos and Mé Féin 2 / Dathanna:** hair and eyes overlap. The app lacks eye colour "liath" and the questions "Cén dath atá ar do shúile?" and "Cén dath atá ar do chuid gruaige?".
- **b5 Scoil and Mé Féin 2:** the app has no "Tá mé ag freastal ar Ardscoil Naomh Pól" and no class or year.
- **b7 Mo lá and the time deck:** the app has "a chlog" and "leath i ndiaidh". The deck adds ceathrú, go dtí, fiche and fiche a cúig.
- **b1 / b9 and Míonna:** the app has every month, but only in the "i mí..." and "de mhí..." forms. The bare names (Eanáir, Samhain, Nollaig) are never taught.
- **Beta grammar lessons:** uimhreacha.json (numbers as labels, sums) and ceisteanna.json (Cé / Cad é + a) partly overlap the numbers and question-word decks, but at grammar level, not as Year 8 content.

**What the decks cover that the Year 8 track lacks entirely:**
- Greetings and goodbyes: "Dia daoibh", "Slán leat" and "Slán agat".
- "Cad é mar atá tú?" and its answers.
- Classroom commands.
- Cardinal numbers 0 to 100.
- Colours beyond hair and eyes.
- Body parts with singular and plural.
- Question words as a set.
- Clock time with quarters and minutes.

### 4.2 Classroom language

**Pupil unit "Sa rang":**
- **An dtig liom?** `An dtig liom (Can I)` + `dul chuig an leithreas (go to the toilet) | deoch uisce a fháil (get a drink of water) | peann a fháil ar iasacht (borrow a pen) | an fhuinneog a oscailt (open the window) | an doras a dhruidim (close the door) | suí anseo (sit here)` + *(opt)* `le do thoil (please)`
- **Mo chuid rudaí:** `Tá (I have) | Níl (I don't have)` + `mo pheann (my pen) | mo leabhar (my book) | mo chóipleabhar (my copybook) | m'obair bhaile (my homework)` + `agam ((at me))`
- **Cabhair** (single column): `Níl a fhios agam (I don't know) | Ní thuigim (I don't understand) | Abair arís é, le do thoil (Say it again, please) | Tá mé réidh (I'm finished) | Tá brón orm go bhfuil mé mall (Sorry I'm late) | Go raibh maith agat (Thank you)`
- **Cad é an Ghaeilge atá ar...?** (What is the Irish for...?) and **Cad é an Béarla atá ar...?** (What is the English for...?), taught as fixed phrases.

**Teacher command card** (a reference and audio deck rather than a builder). Each command is given to one pupil, then to the class:

| To one pupil | To the class | English |
|---|---|---|
| Seas suas | Seasaigí suas | Stand up |
| Suigh síos | Suígí síos | Sit down |
| Éist | Éistigí | Listen |
| Bí ciúin | Bígí ciúin | Be quiet |

| Gabh amach | Gabhaigí amach | Go out |
| Amharc ar an chlár bán | Amharcaigí ar an chlár bán | Look at the whiteboard |

| Druid do leabhar | Druidigí bhur leabhair | Close your book(s) |

  - `Tá sé` + `cúig (five) | deich (ten) | ceathrú (quarter) | fiche (twenty) | fiche cúig (twenty-five) | leath (half)` + `i ndiaidh (past)` + hours

### 4.4 Unit "Uimhreacha 0 go 100"

- **0 go 10** (recognise): `a náid | a haon | a dó | a trí | a ceathair | a cúig | a sé | a seacht | a hocht | a naoi | a deich`
- **11 go 19** (recognise, as full chunks so "a dó dhéag" is always right): `a haon déag | a dó dhéag | a trí déag | ... | a naoi déag`
- **Na deicheanna:** `fiche | tríocha | daichead | caoga | seasca | seachtó | ochtó | nócha` + *(opt)* `a haon ... a naoi`. Any combination is valid, and about 17 audio clips cover 20 to 99.
- **Leathanach / seomra:** `Leathanach (Page) | Seomra (Room)` + `a haon ... a naoi déag`; and `Leathanach | Seomra` + tens + *(opt)* units. This teaches that "a" comes before 1 to 19 but not before fiche.
- **Céad agus míle:** `céad (a hundred) | míle (a thousand) | milliún (a million)`, with "Céad míle fáilte" as the hook. The teacher used that idea; recreate it without his image.
- **Listening game:** reading out phone numbers and car registrations, as in the teacher's slide 59, but with numbers from the Ofcom fiction range.

### 4.5 Unit "Dathanna"

- Questions: "Cad é an dath is fearr leat?" and "Cén dath atá ar do mhála scoile?"
- **An dath is fearr liom:** `Is fearr liom an dath (My favourite colour is)` + `dearg | gorm | glas | buí | dubh | bán | donn | liath | oráiste | bándearg | corcra | dúghorm (navy) | marún (maroon) | órga (gold)`
- **Mo chuid rudaí:** `Tá mo mhála scoile (My school bag is) | Tá mo gheansaí scoile (My school jumper is) | Tá mo rothar (My bike is) | Tá mo bhróga (My shoes are)` + `dúghorm | gorm | dearg | liath | glas | bándearg | corcra | oráiste` + *(opt)* `agus bán (and white) | agus dubh (and black)`. The adjective comes after "tá", so it never lenites or agrees. Black and white are kept out of the first colour list so a pupil can't build "dubh agus dubh".

### 4.6 Unit "An corp"

- **Dhá + singular:** `Tá` + `dhá shúil (two eyes) | dhá chluas (two ears) | dhá lámh (two hands) | dhá chos (two legs/feet) | dhá ghlúin (two knees)` + `agam (I have)`
- **An t-arracht** (the monster): `Tá` + `trí shúil (three eyes) | ceithre chluas (four ears) | cúig lámh (five hands) | sé chos (six legs) | seacht srón (seven noses) | ocht gcloigeann (eight heads)` + `ag an arracht (the monster has)`. This teaches singular nouns after numbers, lenition after 3 to 6 and eclipsis after 7 to 10.
- **Tinn:** `Tá mo (My)` + `cheann (head) | dhroim (back) | chos (leg/foot) | lámh (hand/arm) | bholg (stomach) | sceadamán (throat)` + `tinn (is sore)`
- **Tinneas:** `Tá tinneas (I have a ... ache)` + `cinn (head) | fiacaile (tooth) | cluaise (ear) | droma (back)` + `orm ((on me))`
- **Ghortaigh mé:** `Ghortaigh mé mo (I hurt my)` + `chos | lámh | ghlúin | ghualainn | mhéar` + *(opt)* `ag imirt peile (playing football) | sa chlós (in the yard) | inné (yesterday)`
- **Vocabulary set** from the teacher's list, with a singular and plural sorting game that delivers his "classifying" intention: súil/súile, cluas/cluasa, lámh/lámha, cos/cosa, glúin/glúine, fiacail/fiacla, méar/méara, ordóg/ordóga, leiceann/leicne, mala/malaí, gualainn/guaillí. Leave out sínteog and ladhar until they are checked. Add muineál (neck), bolg (stomach) and uillinn (elbow).
