# Gan Ainm: the plan after the legacy materials

27 September 2026. Built from nine agent reports on your notes, 112 decks, the Easter-course booklets and your worksheets (all in `data/legacy-decks/` and `data/old-grammar-notes/`). Nothing below is built until you approve it.

## Your house style, updated for 2026

Kept from your decks, because it is how you teach:

- **The question is the heading.** Pupils hear the examiner's question first, then build the answer.
- **One idea per screen**, with a picture carrying the meaning rather than English.
- **The part that changes is marked.** Red for letters added to a word (the h, the eclipsis, the ending), green for the particle (An, Ní, go, gur), always underlined as well, so colour is never the only signal.
- **Question, yes and no travel together.** "Ar ith tú? D'ith mé. Níor ith mé."
- **Build to a paragraph, then improve it:** basic, better, best, with the new words in red ("Can you do better?").
- **Bring it home:** Bessbrook, Camlough, Newry, the school, Loch nEathach.
- **Sprioc foghlama at the start, Dul siar at the end, and a proverb to close.**

Updated for 2026: clean screens, large type, dark mode, our own illustrations and local photos instead of clipart and stock images, Áine's voice on every line, tap-to-reveal instead of fly-in animation, and every slide linked to pupil practice.
